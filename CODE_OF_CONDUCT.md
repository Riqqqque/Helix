# Community Conduct

Helix welcomes people who are here to improve the project in good faith. Treat contributors, users, maintainers, and security reporters with patience and respect.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing the work rather than attacking the person;
- giving specific, actionable technical feedback;
- making room for different experience levels and communication styles;
- respecting privacy and coordinated vulnerability disclosure;
- correcting mistakes plainly and accepting reasonable correction;
- keeping disagreement focused on evidence, tradeoffs, and user safety.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate humiliation, doxxing, repeated bad-faith disruption, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community or the project. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters or affected people unnecessarily.

Report conduct problems through a private maintainer contact published for the project. Until that contact exists, do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue; use the repository host's private abuse-reporting channel. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
